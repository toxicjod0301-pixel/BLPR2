// SECTION 01 : ABOUT US

WE DON'T LEAVE MOMENTS TO CHANCE. WE ENGINEER THEM.

Bold Creative Vision Meets Precision Execution

Every landmark event begins with a master plan. The Blueprint Event Management was founded in Khulna to transform ambitious concepts into seamlessly executed concerts, corporate galas, brand activations, and luxury celebrations.

Led by Founding Panel members Abdullah Bin Murshed Fattah and Nibir Guha Roy alongside our Executive Command team, we engineer every stage, sound check, lighting cue, and VIP pass with zero guesswork.

Zero-Failure Redundancy & Show Calling

Every production runs on pre-drafted CAD floorplans, 3-phase backup power readiness, ring-out acoustic soundchecks, and real-time cue-to-cue show direction.

Khulna HQ & Nationwide Deployment

Serving Khulna Club, Hotel Castle Salam, City Inn, CSS Ava Center, KUET, Khulna University, and destination venues across Jessore, Mongla, and Dhaka.
